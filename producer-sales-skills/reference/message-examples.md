# Примеры сообщений

Образцы тона, а не шаблоны для копирования. В каждом реальном сообщении — конкретика из переписки вместо `[...]`. Цены — только из `offers.md`.
Все сообщения без пунктуации (см. `voice.md`). Каждая строка `>` — отдельный пузырь.

## Холодный заход

**Instagram DM (после того как реально послушал трек):**
> yo the bounce on [track] is crazy

> you working on anything new rn

**Instagram DM (артист ищет биты в сторис/посте):**
> saw you looking for beats for the next project

> what kind of sound you going for

**Email:**
> subject: beats for [project name]
>
> hey [name] caught [track] on spotify and the way you ride the second verse is smooth
> I make r&b and trap soul beats in that lane
> want me to send 3 that fit your sound
> no strings just see if anything clicks
>
> [producer name]

(В email `subject:` — служебная пометка темы, сама тема без пунктуации.)

## Квалификация (по ходу разговора)
> that's dope

> when you trying to drop it

> what'd you do to push the last one

> you handling the business side yourself or you got a manager

## Оффер (после того как известны цель и таймлайн)
**Бит:**
> the unlimited lease on this one is $100

> you get mp3 + wav unlimited streams shows videos and 100 percent of the profit stays yours

> I send a contract so everything's official

**Апсейл после «да»:**
> if you want full control on the mix it's +$50 for all the stems

**Эксклюзив (если спросил):**
> exclusive starts from $400

> you get full ownership the beat comes out of my catalog and never gets sold again

> mp3 + wav + all the stems and we sign an exclusive contract under your name

## Follow-up

**После оффера, 2–3 дня тишины:**
> yo you get a chance to run the beat through

> curious what you came up with

**С новым поводом:**
> just made one that reminded me of [his reference artist]

> want me to send it

**Невыполненное обещание (сначала выполнить, потом писать):**
> my bad for the wait here's the pack I said I'd send [link/files from producer]

**Закрывающее (после 3–4 касаний без ответа):**
> gonna stop hitting you about this so I'm not clogging your dms

> if you ever need beats for a drop I got you

**Возобновление (60–90 дней, реальный повод):**
> saw you just dropped [new track] congrats

> that one's a vibe

> you got another one lined up

## Возражения

**«too expensive»:**
> I hear you

> what were you expecting to spend on it

> I'd rather find something that works than lose you over it

**«let me think about it»:**
> for sure

> is it more the price or not sure the beat's the one

**«I'll get back to you when I get paid»:**
> bet when do you usually get paid

> I'll hold it till then

**«is this legit?»:**
> fair question there's a lot of scams out here

> every lease comes with a signed contract and you can check my page first

> what would make you feel good about it
